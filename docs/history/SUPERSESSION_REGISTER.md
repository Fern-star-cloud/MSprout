Latest task and publication evidence belong in CURRENT_STATE/ROADMAP_STATUS and the actual finalization receipt.

This register records those approvals; it does not invent separate approval of unresolved choices. Repository versions of the design and roadmap transcribe the accepted baseline with those labels intact. Governance changes beyond the reviewed DOC-GOV-01 amendments still require separate review. The prior audit handoff and planning assignment are source context, not additional execution permissions.

## Naming and conflict rules

- **MVP Tasks 1–18**, **UI-01–UI-16**, **UX-03–UX-08**, **Packs 01–12**, and **modernization tasks** are distinct namespaces. Preserve their historical numbering and commit evidence.
- UX-03 is Home/branding; UX-04 is shared navigation. Entry/authentication may be considered within the approved UX-F2 scope but is not retroactively renamed UX-03.
- Later approval may supersede a visual instruction without changing storage, API, security or business rules. Apply supersession only to its stated subject.
- Approved fourth-state direction does not approve an attendance formula, guest rule, bulk Late action, purple styling, feature header, HTTP status, restart procedure or activation strategy.
- Report N/A for no finalized records is approved display direction; the proposed rate formula and additional zero-denominator cases remain explicitly proposed.
- Governance edits do not grant themselves finalization authority. Section 14 remains controlling; the stakeholder separately authorized the reviewed DOC-GOV-01 finalization, not future governance changes.

## Preservation and future maintenance

Original source bodies, dated approval records, QA evidence and completed-task history remain in their original files. Notices clarify authority without rewriting the historical account. External references have not been copied into the repository. Missing external sources are not broken repository links and must not be replaced with fabricated content.

Future supersession entries must identify the stakeholder decision, affected subject, retained requirements, replacement source, effective scope and authorization limits. Material unresolved conflicts stop the affected operation under AGENTS.md; document maintenance must not silently settle them.
